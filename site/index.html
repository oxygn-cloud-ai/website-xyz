Asked and answered

What is Oxygn?

An AI workforce for governance, risk, compliance, legal operations, internal audit and company secretarial work. AI specialists do the work continuously, supervisors route and check it, and a qualified professional signs off everything that matters.

Who makes the decisions?

People. The workforce drafts, checks and escalates. A qualified person reads the work, amends it where needed and approves it. Nothing reaches a regulator without that approval.

What are the registers on this page?

Real ones, kept for a client and shown with their details removed: 275 artefacts across eight registers, assessed around the clock. The largest is a risk register of 168 risks, each with a priority, a probability and an impact.

Where does our data live?

In your own instance: a separate workforce, encryption keys, data boundary and audit ledger for each client.

Can our regulator see the work?

Yes. Evidence and controls are available to you and your supervisor on request, and every action records what was done, by which agent, and who approved it.

Which functions can it take on?

Governance, risk, compliance, legal operations, internal audit and company secretarial work. The six are listed above with what each covers.
